# 生图提示词模板

每张图单独生成。根据内容替换变量，不要把多张图拼在一起。

```text
Create one standalone 16:9 horizontal Chinese article illustration for the user's 小vi IP.

Visual style:
Pure white background. Minimalist flat hand-drawn illustration. Thick rounded near-black linework. Clean simple color blocks. Lots of empty white space. Cute, clean, warm, and personal-brand friendly. No gradients, no shadows, no paper texture, no 3D, no realistic portrait, no commercial vector polish, no PPT infographic look, no complex background.

Character:
小vi, a simplified cute girl with a very round soft face, tiny black dot eyes, tiny U-shaped smile, round Claude-coral blush dots, long dark warm-brown hair as one simple rounded shape, a tiny Claude-coral cherry hair clip, and a simple cream-white ruffled collar/dress. 小vi must perform the core conceptual action, not decorate the scene.

Color palette:
Pure white background #FFFFFF. Near-black linework #191919. Dark warm-brown hair #3B2E26. Warm cream #FFF8EF. Claude Coral #D97757 only for the cherry hair clip, cheeks, main path/arrows, and sparse highlight marks. Do not use blue, green, purple, pink, neon, or multicolor details.

Theme:
{主题}

Core idea:
{核心意思}

Composition:
{具体画面：小vi在哪里、正在做什么、主要物件是什么、信息如何流动}

Suggested elements:
{元素1} / {元素2} / {元素3} / {元素4}

Optional short labels:
{短标注1} / {短标注2} / {短标注3}

Constraints:
One image explains only one core structure. Keep the main subject around 40%-60% of the canvas. Preserve at least 35% blank white space. Use no readable text unless explicitly requested. Do not write a title in the top-left corner. Do not make it a formal diagram, course slide, dense explainer, 3D render, or realistic portrait. Do not copy prior examples directly; invent a fresh visual metaphor for this specific content.
```
